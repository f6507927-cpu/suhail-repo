Strings requiring review include:

- Research workflow headings, draft queue, request/inspection labels, empty and request-feedback states.
- The static P2 warning that research prompts will not go live until O13 is approved.
- Research validation/failure, field-by-field editing, AI-vs-edited diff labels, citation and risk-coverage labels.
- Human-readable draft prose section titles, workflow states, and blocking/warning labels.
- All seven edit-reason labels and six analyst checklist statements.
- Evidence packet item/source availability and source-opening labels.
- Regeneration guidance and workflow action/status labels.
- Scope/evidence settings fields, sector controls, and P2 publication gate warning and evidence labels.
