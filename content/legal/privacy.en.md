# Privacy Policy (English)

Draft for legal review. Version draft-2026-10-11, last updated 22 October 2026. This is a translation; the Arabic text prevails.

## 1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is Suhail, operated by its founding team, based in Cairo, Egypt. Company registration is pending. Privacy contact: suhailroboadvisor@gmail.com.

## 2. Data we collect

If you browse without an account: we do not ask for any personal data. We use essential cookies only (section 4). Our hosting providers may automatically record technical data, such as IP address and browser type, to operate and secure the service.

If you create an account:

- your Google account ID, name and email address;
- sign-in timestamps;
- the version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted, and when;
- an audit log of actions on your account (such as creation, sign-in and deletion).

Later, when phone sign-in is added: your mobile number, to send a one-time code by WhatsApp.

We do not collect sensitive data, financial data or payment data.

## 3. Why we use your data

- To create your account, sign you in and manage it.
- To prove that you accepted the Terms and Privacy Policy.
- To protect accounts and the site, and investigate misuse.
- To comply with the law and respond to requests from competent authorities.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not track you across other sites.

## 4. Cookies

We use essential cookies only:

- to keep you signed in;
- to remember your theme and language preference.

We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

## 5. Who we share your data with

We share your data only with service providers that process it on our behalf and under our instructions:

| Provider | Service | Processing location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database, authentication, file storage | India (Mumbai) |
| Replit | Website hosting | United States |
| Google | Sign-in | United States |

We may disclose data where a law or a competent authority requires it.

We plan to use Anthropic later to write AI explanations of screening results. We will not send it any personal data.

## 6. Transfers outside the Kingdom

Your data is processed by providers outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, as shown in the table above. We do so in line with the Personal Data Protection Law and the Regulation on Personal Data Transfer Outside the Kingdom.

## 7. Retention and deletion

- We keep your account data while your account exists.
- You can delete your account yourself from the account page. Deletion removes your personal data; audit records keep only a reference that does not identify you.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including restricting access and encrypting connections to the site. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.

## 9. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to:

- be informed how your data is collected and used (this policy is part of that);
- access your data and obtain a copy;
- correct, complete or update your data;
- request destruction of your data, which you can do directly by deleting your account;
- withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any right, email suhailroboadvisor@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA).

## 10. Minors

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy. We will publish the new version on the site with its date, and ask account holders to accept it where required.

## 12. Language

If the Arabic text and any translation differ, the Arabic text prevails.
