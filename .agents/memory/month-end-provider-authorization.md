description: Founder boundaries and licensing restrictions for future provider work.
---

API Ninjas work is authorized for preparation and mocked testing only, not live
requests, Supabase access, applying migrations (even tests), hosted configuration,
schedules or activation. The founder supplies their own key.

**Why:** The founder explicitly limited execution. The provider's free tier
prohibits commercial use; attribution alone is not provider permission.

**How to apply:** Read D-153 and docs/API_NINJAS_MONTH_END.md before any provider
continuation. Seek separate founder authorization and suitable provider permission
before live commercial use; never silently substitute a paid/live feed. Keep the
storage rollout off until the separately authorized migration is verified; key
presence is not schema readiness.
