---
name: Arabic review draft boundary
description: Founder instruction for future Arabic review inventories and bounded approvals.
---

The founder said: “From now on, every review inventory must include an actual
Arabic draft for each row. A row with only English is a gap to fill, not a draft.”
Do not approve rows whose draft is only the English source. Legal bodies remain
exceptions requiring founder-supplied, legally reviewed O-7 text.

**Why:** The founder's follow-up approved a bounded set of supplied phrases,
not the entire spreadsheet or its English-only draft rows.

**How to apply:** Keep documentation proposals separate from runtime approvals
and approval cells. Preserve earlier approvals unless the founder explicitly
supersedes them. Never invent legal-body drafts to make an inventory look complete.
